Nearby Amenities in Waterfall, North Las Vegas

Explore dining, grocery, parks, golf, healthcare, and schools around the Waterfall community in ZIP 89085—plus approximate drive times to major destinations in the Las Vegas Valley.

Interactive Map

Dining & Entertainment

The Aliante area puts a wide range of restaurants and casual dining within a short drive of Waterfall. Aliante Casino + Hotel on Aliante Parkway hosts multiple restaurants, a movie theater, and entertainment options that many North Las Vegas residents use for nights out without driving to the Strip.

Grocery & Daily Errands

Smith's Food and Drug (6855 Aliante Pkwy, North Las Vegas) and Albertsons (3010 W Ann Rd, North Las Vegas) are established supermarkets serving the northwest valley. Additional retail and services cluster along Ann Road, Aliante Parkway, and the 215 Beltway corridor.

Parks & Recreation

Inside Waterfall, residents enjoy HOA amenities including a resort-style pool, spa, fitness center, pickleball courts, and walking trails. Nearby, Aliante Nature Discovery Park (2627 Nature Park Dr) offers trails, playgrounds, and open space a short drive from the community.

Golf

Aliante Golf Club (3100 W Elkhorn Rd) is a public course with practice facilities close to Waterfall—convenient tee times in North Las Vegas for residents who golf locally.

Healthcare

Centennial Hills Hospital Medical Center (6900 N Durango Dr, Las Vegas) is a major acute-care hospital northwest of Waterfall. Urgent care, primary care, and pharmacy locations are also available along Durango Drive and the Ann Road retail corridor; confirm hours and services before you go.

Shopping

Day-to-day shopping is centered on grocery anchors and strip retail near Aliante and Ann Road. For larger regional malls, many Waterfall residents drive toward Summerlin or the Las Vegas Strip depending on what they need.

Schools

Which CCSD schools are assigned to Waterfall addresses? Assignments depend on your exact street address in the 89085 area. Verify with the CCSD Zoning Search before you make a decision based on school boundaries.
